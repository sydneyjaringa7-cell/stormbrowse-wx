Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how StormBrowse WX handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

StormBrowse WX may process information you choose to provide, app preferences, and local browsing data such as history, saved pages, and downloads. In this standalone version, profile information is stored on your device.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide browser features, remember your settings, open links, show weather tools, send notifications if enabled, and respond to support requests.

3. Local Data

StormBrowse WX stores settings and optional profile information locally on your device. You can clear local browsing data from the Privacy tools.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services that process information used to provide their features. These may include:

  • Google Play Services, when provided by the app platform
  • External websites opened from StormBrowse WX
  • Weather, map, search, and sharing services selected by you

Third-party websites have their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We value your trust and use commercially reasonable means of protecting information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Children’s Privacy

StormBrowse WX is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the app support channel so it can be reviewed.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app’s contact feature.
By using StormBrowse WX, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
